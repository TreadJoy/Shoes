# A2.3 Plan 2 — Provider, Region, and Recovery Decision

**Decision ID:** `A2.3-P2`
**Prepared:** 2026-08-31
**Status:** **Approved for the private POC; Free database and private Render web origin provisioned, public edge remains pending**
**Scope:** Provider and recovery evaluation plus the owner-approved disposable
Free-Postgres database and development-team-only Render web origin. No paid
resource, durable data, DNS, storage, or public deployment is authorized by
this record.

## Confirmed requirements

The owner confirmed that the fixed A2.3 architecture remains in force:

- Research hostname: `research.tread-joy.com`.
- Default media hostname: `media.research.tread-joy.com`.
- Cloudflare remains the edge; the application and PostgreSQL remain on a
  managed origin.
- Research and commerce retain separate databases, secrets, media, and
  deployment configuration.
- The private POC must be reachable only by the development team. Public
  exposure is a later gate.
- The provider must support Python 3.12 WSGI serving, secrets, scheduled jobs,
  origin restriction, PostgreSQL TLS, isolated restore, export/exit, and
  monitoring/alert integration.
- Recovery objectives are RTO no greater than one hour and RPO no greater than
  fifteen minutes. The maximum backup window is 35 days.
- The POC has one assigned owner and one temporary mailbox,
  `ethan@tread-joy.com`; consumer broadcast/marketing mail is out of scope.

The 35-day backup window is an engineering acceptance target, not evidence that
any candidate's default plan meets it. A provider's native window may need to be
extended with encrypted, separately retained logical exports.

## Approved private-POC decision

The owner approved the following on 2026-08-31:

1. **Provider:** Render.
2. **Workspace/application tier:** Hobby workspace and Free web service for
   the private POC. This is suitable only for testing: the service can sleep
   after inactivity, has an ephemeral filesystem, and lacks several paid
   operational features.
3. **Database tier:** Render Free Postgres (`free`) with its fixed 1 GB
   capacity. The owner selected this disposable tier on 2026-09-03 for the
   private POC after reviewing the live dashboard's $0/month total. It expires
   after 30 days, has no provider backups/PITR or managed connection pooling,
   and may restart for maintenance. Use only synthetic or owner test data; do
   not represent this tier as durable research infrastructure. Upgrade to the
   paid `0.1c-256mb` tier (or a separately approved alternative) before storing
   personal/research data or relying on recovery targets.
4. **Region:** `ohio` for both the Django service and PostgreSQL. Render does
   not offer a Wisconsin region; Ohio is the selected nearest available
   managed region to the owner's Wisconsin preference. Region changes require
   creating and migrating to new resources.
5. **Marketing/add-ons:** no advertising, consumer outreach, marketing
   broadcasts, or related paid service is enabled. Provider-native cron and
   monitoring may be evaluated later, but no paid add-on is authorized by this
   decision.
6. **Backup destination:** Free Postgres provides no provider backup or PITR.
   Optional encrypted logical exports may be used outside the repository for
   portability only; they do not establish the one-hour RTO or fifteen-minute
   RPO. Backup credentials, archives, and encryption keys must never be
   committed. Upgrade to a paid database and complete an isolated restore
   drill before any durable research data is accepted.
7. **Incident/operations responder:** the project owner.

The original 2026-08-31 decision authorized documentation and provider-specific
implementation planning only. The 2026-09-03 amendment below separately
authorizes the $0 Free-Postgres POC provisioning; paid upgrades, paid add-ons,
DNS changes, and public exposure remain gated and require an exact
price/configuration review before creation.

### Private-POC database amendment — 2026-09-03

The owner superseded the paid-database choice above for the initial private
POC and authorized Render Free Postgres at the dashboard's $0/month total.
This does not relax the data boundary: the database is disposable, expires
after 30 days, has no provider backup/PITR, and must not hold customer or other
durable personal data. Any upgrade, paid add-on, DNS change, or public exposure
remains a separate approval gate.

The public research launch still requires reevaluating the sleeping Free web
service, replacing laptop-dependent backups with an approved off-site design,
verifying provider terms/data handling, proving team-only Cloudflare access and
origin lock-down, and completing every public gate in Plan 1.

### Live provider evidence — 2026-09-03

Render now contains the owner-approved `treadjoy-research-staging-db` in Ohio:
Free plan, PostgreSQL 17, fixed 1 GB, `$0/month`, and expiration on October 3,
2026. The database-level inbound allowlist is empty and Render confirms that
external traffic is blocked. No paid upgrade, add-on, web service, database
role, migration, email, scheduler, DNS, R2, or public exposure was created.
The provider-managed owner credential remains only in Render and is not
recorded here.

### Plan 7 cost and capability amendment — 2026-09-04

The follow-on provider/cost gate is recorded in
[`docs/a2.3-provider-cost-gate.md`](a2.3-provider-cost-gate.md). It preserves
the owner's `$0` disposable POC decision but records two incompatibilities that
must not be hidden by provisioning: Render Free cannot provide the fixed
recovery contract or the planned shell/scheduler/email path, and Cloudflare
Free/Pro cannot express the exact A2.2 600-second host/method/source-IP rules.
Cloudflare Business is the lowest documented network tier that can express
those rules. Plan 8 therefore remains stopped until the owner explicitly
chooses to defer those gates or approves the compatible paid path after an
exact dashboard charge review.
