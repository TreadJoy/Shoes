# Join The Sole And Structural Chassis As A Permanent Core Assembly

> Superseded for current product/catalog taxonomy by ADR 0003. The engineering
> statements below remain revision-specific historical evidence; this record
> does not establish the current seven-group visual BOM or prove physical
> attachment/removal behavior.

The owner approved a permanent union of the sole, carrier, bootie, structural chassis, and fit-critical reinforcement for Heritage Court V3 engineering development. This product contract supersedes the earlier concept of a customer-removable M08 sole. Because TreadJoy reserves **module** for customer-interchangeable assemblies, the union is named **Permanent Core Assembly** and identified as `TJ-HC3-C01`.

## Consequences

- `TJ-HC3-M08` is retired as a customer-interchangeable module and is replaced by `TJ-HC3-C01`; the sole remains an internal BOM subassembly of the permanent core.
- The active interchangeable modules are M09 Integrated Upper Overlay, M04 Back Panel and Heel, M05 Tongue, M06 Art Badge, and M07 Laces.
- Removable-sole rails, toe hooks, releases, gaskets, and related interface rigs are not carried into engineering CAD.
- Customer swaps are off-foot and unloaded. Releases must be deliberate, guarded, and concealed; laces must be removed before M09 or M05 when they obstruct removal.
- Right US men's 9 is the only dimension-controlled development size. Fit-critical interior, carrier, and last surfaces cannot be frozen until an engineer/manufacturer-reviewed last is acquired with units, revision, and use rights.
- The Blender R16 visual geometry remains design evidence, not manufacturing CAD. Existing source files and historical evidence remain unchanged.

## First Physical Gate

Before a full permanent core is modeled, test representative M09 load-path coupons for toe capture, eyelet-row load transfer, quarter anchoring, guarded release behavior, fit, misassembly, and wear. Coupon dimensions are calibration variables until physical results are recorded.
