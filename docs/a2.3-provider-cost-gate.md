### A2.2 edge-policy incompatibility

The checked-in and owner-approved A2.2 policy requires two rules: `POST` to
the waitlist and survey paths, counted by source IP, on the fixed research
host, over a 600-second period, with staging `log` and production
`managed_challenge` actions. Cloudflare's current rate-limiting matrix says:

- Free has one rule, a 10-second period, and only Path/Verified Bot expression
  fields.
- Pro has two rules and periods up to one minute, but still does not expose the
  required Method and Source IP fields.
- Business has five rules, periods up to ten minutes, and includes Host, Method,
  and Source IP fields.

Therefore the exact A2.2 policy cannot be deployed on Free or Pro. The lowest
Cloudflare network tier that can express it is Business. Cloudflare also
documents a challenge-action duration nuance: Free/Pro/Business cannot select
an arbitrary challenge duration, so the live managed-challenge behavior must
be confirmed before claiming the 600-second mitigation semantics.

This is a hard gate, not a reason to silently weaken the approved rule. The
owner must either approve Business, approve a documented A2.2 policy change, or
keep the POC local/disposable and defer Plan 8.

## Cost paths and decision

### Path A — retain the `$0` disposable POC

Render Free web/Postgres, Cloudflare Free plus free Access, blank R2 Standard,
and Resend Free can be kept at a documented `$0` service total before usage.
This path is appropriate for synthetic data and owner learning only. It does
not provide the fixed recovery contract, exact A2.2 rate rules, unattended
jobs, SMTP from the free web service, or durable staging evidence. No live
Cloudflare/R2/email resource should be created until the owner accepts those
limits and chooses to defer the corresponding gates.

### Path B — satisfy the fixed recovery and edge requirements

The planning baseline is approximately **`$213/month` on annual billing**:
about `$13/month` for the lowest paid Render application/database baseline,
plus Cloudflare Business at `$200/month`, with Resend Free and blank R2 still
within their free allowances. Monthly Cloudflare billing would make that
approximately **`$263/month`**. These totals exclude usage, storage/bandwidth,
an external 35-day encrypted export destination, possible `$1/month` cron,
taxes, and any provider-specific charges. The exact Render dashboard total
must be recorded before purchase; this document is not an authorization to
incur it.

## Gate outcome

1. Render/Ohio and the owner-only mailbox decision are confirmed.
2. The Free Render database already provisioned remains disposable and
   correctly blocked from durable use.
3. Resend Free and Cloudflare Access Free are cost-compatible with the private
   owner-only preview, but live service configuration is still absent.
4. Cloudflare Free/Pro cannot implement the exact A2.2 edge contract; Business
   is the lowest matching tier.
5. The lowest Render tiers do not prove RTO ≤1 hour, RPO ≤15 minutes, or a
   35-day backup window.
6. **Plan 8 is blocked** until the owner chooses Path A (explicitly defer the
   incompatible gates) or Path B (approve the paid compatible path and its
   exact dashboard charges), or approves a new architecture/policy decision.

No provider settings, credentials, DNS, R2 bucket, email domain, paid service,
or deployment state changed during this gate.
