# TreadJoy Product Language

This glossary defines the product terms used to describe Heritage Court's modular concept and its visual development.

## Language

**Integrated Upper Overlay**:
The single connected outer-shell assembly spanning the dark forefoot surround, lower strip, connectors, eyestay bands, and side overlay, including its mounted eyelets and overlay stitching.
_Avoid_: Separate forefoot module, eyestay module, quarter module, three-piece outer shell

**Permanent Chassis**:
The structural base intended to retain and support the foot independently of replaceable styling assemblies; the warm-stone vamp and quarter belong to this base.
_Avoid_: Extra module, removable quarter base

**Permanent Core Assembly**:
The non-customer-removable union of sole, carrier, bootie, structural chassis, and fit-critical reinforcement. The sole remains an internal BOM subassembly; it is not an interchangeable module.
_Avoid_: Sole module, removable sole, seventh module, sole release

**Module**:
A named product assembly intended to be interchangeable as a unit; the term alone does not establish a working attachment or approved removal method.
_Avoid_: Proven removable part, individual mesh

**Art Badge**:
The identity assembly carrying the fixed TreadJoy mark, with coordinated artwork, backing, or finish variants.
_Avoid_: New logo for each colorway

**Art Kit**:
A coordinated set of surface patterns, colors, and finishes applied to the same shoe geometry.
_Avoid_: New shoe model, production color standard

**Visual Master**:
The reviewed digital shoe used to produce concept imagery, whose appearance is distinct from engineered dimensions and physical performance.
_Avoid_: Manufacturing master, printable shoe, validated prototype

**Studio Lineup**:
A presentation of coordinated or mixed module styles that communicates the concept without asserting an assembly sequence or removal path.
_Avoid_: Exploded mechanism demonstration
